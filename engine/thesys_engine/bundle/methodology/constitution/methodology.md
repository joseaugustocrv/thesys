# Thesys Engineering Constitution

## Purpose

Thesys defines a controlled engineering system for turning human intent into
verified, traceable and maintainable software. It can be used with Agile,
Scrum, Kanban, DevOps or other delivery models.

## Non-negotiable principles

1. Intent precedes implementation.
2. Authoritative decisions are explicit and attributable.
3. AI output is proposal material until accepted through the applicable gate.
4. Material changes propagate from the source artifact to derived artifacts.
5. Significant engineering claims require evidence appropriate to their risk.
6. Security, quality, operability and maintainability are engineering concerns,
   not release-time afterthoughts.
7. Human accountability is retained for business intent, acceptance, risk
   acceptance, architectural decisions and release decisions.
8. Large systems are decomposed into recursively manageable engineering units.
9. The smallest sufficient artifact set is used, but mandatory controls cannot
   be bypassed merely to reduce documentation.
10. Exceptions are explicit, time-bounded where appropriate, and supported by
    risk acceptance.
